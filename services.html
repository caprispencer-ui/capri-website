Questions

Where do sessions take place?

Anywhere in Utah County. Lakeshores, open fields and mountain spots all work, and you can suggest your own location when you book. A travel fee is added for locations farther away.

How long until I get my photos?

Photos are delivered within 1 to 2 weeks of your session.

How many photos will I get?

Individual and senior sessions include 60+ photos, family sessions 65+, couples 70+ and branding sessions 75+.

Do you photograph school dance groups?

Yes. School dance pricing starts at $15 and depends on how many couples are in your group.

How do I book?

Fill out the session inquiry form with your session type, date and location, or message me on Instagram @captured.by.capri. For anything not listed here, ask for a custom quote.
