Questions

How much does a senior photo session cost?

Senior and individual sessions with captured by capri are $75 and include 60+ photos. Couples sessions are $90 (70+ photos), family sessions $105 (65+ photos) and branding sessions $115 (75+ photos).

Where do sessions take place?

Anywhere in Utah County, including Provo, Orem, Lehi, American Fork, Pleasant Grove, Spanish Fork, Springville, Saratoga Springs, Eagle Mountain and nearby towns. Lakeshores, open fields and mountain spots all work, and you can suggest your own location when you book. A travel fee is added for locations farther away.

How long until I get my photos?

Photos are delivered within 1 to 2 weeks of your session.

How many photos will I get?

Individual and senior sessions include 60+ photos, family sessions 65+, couples 70+ and branding sessions 75+.

Do you photograph school dance groups?

Yes. School dance pricing starts at $15 and depends on how many couples are in your group.

What should I wear for my photo session?

Wear a cute outfit you feel confident and comfortable in. Solid colors and soft, neutral tones photograph beautifully, and for couples and families it helps to coordinate colors without matching exactly.

What time of day is best for photos?

Golden hour, the hour or so before sunset, is my favorite. The light is soft and warm and looks amazing in Utah County's mountains, fields and lakeshores.

What happens if it rains?

Rainy day shoots can be amazing, so we can keep your session if you're up for it. If you'd rather wait for better weather, we can reschedule.

How do I pay?

Payment is through Venmo.

How do I book?

Fill out the session inquiry form with your session type, date and location, or message me on Instagram @captured.by.capri. For anything not listed here, ask for a custom quote.
